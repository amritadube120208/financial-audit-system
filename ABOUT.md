## 📱 Application Overview

### Purpose & Mission
Our platform is built to deliver a seamless, secure, and transparent workflow for monitoring business activities and operations. By combining centralized auditing with real-time transaction tracking, users gain full visibility into every event and financial movement across the system.

### Key Capabilities

* 🔍 **Comprehensive Audit Logging**
  * Real-time tracking of system events, user actions, and administrative changes.
  * Search, filter, and export detailed logs for compliance and reporting.

* 💳 **Transaction Management**
  * Consolidated view of pending, completed, and flagged transactions on the same interactive interface.
  * Deep-dive status inspection with clear line-item breakdowns.

* 🛡️ **Security & Compliance Standards**
  * Role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring data privacy.
  * Immutable logs and encrypted transaction records for high-reliability operational auditability.
